The Merit Badge Workshop held last July 25 at the Quezon City Council Session Hall was a success, bringing Scouts together to learn, grow, and take another step toward achieving the Explorer, Pathfinder, and Outdoorsman ranks.

COMMUNITY EVENT

National Hopia Day 2026

INFO

National Hopia Day 2026 brought KAB Scouts and Eagle Scouts together at Quezon City High School for a fun and meaningful celebration filled with games, cultural learning, and the spirit of unity, made possible through the generous support of Eng Bee Tin, Mr. Gerry Chua, and their dedicated staff.

JUL 04, 2026
COMMUNITY SERVICE

Small Actions, Big Impact

INFO

Last July 4, 2026, QCC Eagle Scouts came together for a meaningful clean-up drive at the Quezon City Council. Huge shoutout to all our Eagle Scouts who volunteered. Thank you for giving your time and energy to keep our council clean and green. Your dedication and spirit of service truly reflect what Scouting is all about.
