LEADERSHIP THROUGH THE YEARS

Former Presidents

Previous presidents who have served the association throughout its leadership history.

7TH
2022 – 2026

Ms. Genessa Lorre S. Garcia

FIRST FEMALE PRESIDENT

The association’s first female and longest-serving president led the organization during the pandemic. Afterward, the focus shifted to adaptability, rebuilding, and organizational growth.

6TH
2021 – 2022

Mr. Paul James Castor-Untalan

YOUNGEST PRESIDENT

The youngest and shortest-serving ESA president highlighted his term through the establishment and organization of districts, numerous activities, and initiatives that prepared the association for the pandemic.

5TH
2019 – 2021

Mr. John Loyd M. Carpio

THE BIKING PRESIDENT

A president who responds to the needs of the youth by promoting sports activities both within and outside the association, while also focusing on community projects that create opportunities, encourage active participation, and support the growth and development of the community.

4TH
2017 – 2019

Mr. Enrico Rafael B. Patulan

FOUNDING PRESIDENT OF THE QUEZON CITY COUNCIL EAGLE SCOUT ASSOCIATION

During his term, he became the first president of the QCC-ESA, formerly known as ESOQC. His leadership focused on strengthening the association and developing future leaders.

3RD
2016 – 2017

Mr. Sherbin Seth A. Seto

LAST PRESIDENT OF ESOQC

He was the last leader of ESOQC, serving a short term but providing strong guidance and direction before the transition. He embodied the call of the association, bringing its members together and strengthening unity and commitment.

2ND
2014 – 2016

Mr. Marc Zion M. Cresencio

SIMPLE AND HUMBLE PRESIDENT

A simple leader and second president of ESOQC who focused on improving the system, strengthening the organization, and encouraging more Eagle Scouts to join and serve in Quezon City.

1ST
2012 – 2014

Mr. Raoul Abraham Jeuel C. Roldan

First President of ESOQC

During his term, he became the first president of the QCC-ESA, formerly known as ESOQC. His leadership focused on strengthening the association and developing future leaders.

Goal of this Term of Association 2026 – 2028

Growth and Development of

New Generation Eagle Scouts

In this term of association, we aim to develop and nurture the new generation of Eagle Scouts, fostering leadership, community service, and personal growth. Our goal is to support more scouts in achieving the prestigious rank of Eagle Scout, while also providing opportunities for them to engage in meaningful projects and initiatives that benefit our community. We believe that by empowering the next generation of leaders, we can create a lasting impact and uphold the values of scouting for years to come.
