About QCC Eagle Scout Association

The official organization of Eagle Scouts within the Quezon City Council of the Boy Scouts of the Philippines.

Our History

The Quezon City Council Eagle Scout Association (QCC-ESA) was established to unite Eagle Scouts of the Quezon City Council in promoting leadership, service, fellowship, and civic responsibility.

Since its formation, the association has served as a platform for Eagle Scouts to collaborate on community service projects, leadership development initiatives, environmental campaigns, and youth mentoring programs.

QCC-ESA continues to strengthen the Eagle Scout network across the six congressional districts of Quezon City while supporting the mission and ideals of the Boy Scouts of the Philippines.

Mission

To unite Eagle Scouts in advancing leadership, service, and community development through meaningful programs, civic engagement, and fellowship.

Vision

A dynamic community of Eagle Scouts inspiring future generations through excellence, integrity, service, and responsible leadership.
