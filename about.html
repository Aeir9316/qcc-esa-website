OUR FOUNDATION

Preamble

The Quezon City Council Eagle Scout Association is founded on the principles of service, leadership, fellowship, and commitment to community. We bring together Eagle Scouts who continue to live out the values of Scouting through meaningful service, responsible leadership, and lifelong brotherhood.
